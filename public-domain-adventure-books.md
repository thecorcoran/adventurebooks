# Alaskan & Seafaring Public Domain Adventures

A curated bibliography of classic public domain adventure narratives, divided into two comprehensive volumes:
1. **Alaska & Polar Adventures** (Expeditions, Gold Rushes, Homesteading, Hunting, and Arctic/Antarctic Survival)
2. **Sea Stories & Maritime Adventures** (Tall Ships, Whaling, Naval Sagas, Solo Circumnavigations, and Pirate Epics)

All works listed are in the public domain and freely accessible via Project Gutenberg and LibriVox.
